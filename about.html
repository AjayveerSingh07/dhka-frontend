About DHKA.

The Distt. Hoshiarpur Karate Do Association guides karate students across the district through disciplined training, fair gradings and competitive tournaments.

A community built on discipline.

DHKA was founded to give young people in Hoshiarpur a place to train with respect, focus and honor. From first-time beginners to seasoned competitors, every student is guided by experienced instructors.

Today we bring together dojos from across the district, organize belt gradings and tournaments, and prepare our players for state and national competition.

Our mission

To make quality karate training available to every young person in the district, and to build champions who are as respectful as they are skilled.

Our values

Discipline, strength and honor guide everything we do, in the dojo and outside it.
